Standard Input
Students: 0
Format: Name, Number, ProblemType, Count, Grade;
Number can be any text (e.g. 2, 14B, Blue) or left empty. The name may be left empty too — it prints a blank Name line (e.g. , 2, add2, 30, 3;).
You can also paste one student per line.
You can also paste five columns (name, number, problem type, count, grade) straight from a spreadsheet.
Example: Harrison, 1, add2, 30, 3; Emily, 2, mixed, 40, 4;
Problem types: add1–add5, sub1–sub5, mul1–mul5, div1–div5 (Levels 1–5 of addition, subtraction, multiplication, division), fractions, fractions[add|sub|mul|divf|divw], decimals, decimals[add|sub|mulw|muld|divw|divd], rounding, longdivision, orderofoperations, integers, exponents, scientificnotation, mixed, mixed[type1|type2|...]
Counts: 30, 40, 50, 60 — Grades: 1–8
Extra spaces are okay. Blank trailing entries are ignored. Invalid entries are skipped and listed in an error message.
