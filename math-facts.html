Standard Input
Students: 0
Format: STUDENTNAME, NUMBER, FACTCODE; NEXTSTUDENT, NEXTNUMBER, FACTCODE;
Fact codes are listed in the Level Reference.
NUMBER can be any text (e.g. 2, 14B, Blue) or left empty. The name may be left empty too — it prints a blank Name line (e.g. , 2, +10;).
You can also paste one student per line.
You can also paste three columns (name, number, level) straight from a spreadsheet.
Example: Harrison, 2, +10; Emily, 5, x7; Jacob, 14, mix/40;
Extra spaces are okay. Blank trailing entries are ignored. Invalid entries are skipped and listed in an error message.
